# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: March 12, 2026*

## 1. Who We Are

Radar Puls is a personal project by an individual based in Niš, Republic of Serbia. Radar Puls is an informational platform for displaying traffic reports.

**Contact for data protection inquiries:**
Email: lazar.cve@gmail.com

## 2. What Data We Collect

### Technical Data (Automatic)
- IP address (anonymized)
- Browser type and version
- Operating system and device type
- Pages visited on the website and time of visit
- Referral URL (the website you came from)

### Location Data
- Approximate location based on IP address
- Precise location ONLY with explicit user consent (for map display)
- Location data is not stored permanently

### Cookies and Local Storage
- Cookie consent preference (localStorage)
- Theme selection — light/dark (localStorage)
- Language selection (session cookie)
- See our Cookie Policy for details

### Data Voluntarily Provided by User
- Email address (if subscribing to newsletter — in future versions)

## 3. Purpose of Data Processing

We use data exclusively for:
- **Displaying traffic information** — core service function
- **Service improvement** — usage analysis to enhance user experience
- **Anonymous analytics** — understanding website traffic (visitor count, popular pages)
- **Security** — protection against abuse and technical issues

We do not use data for profiling, targeted advertising, or selling to third parties.

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing

In accordance with the Law on Personal Data Protection of the Republic of Serbia (ZZPL), we process data based on:
- **Legitimate interest** — for service operation and maintenance, security, analytics (Article 12, Paragraph 1, Point 6 of ZZPL)
- **Consent** — for non-essential cookies, location data, newsletter (Article 12, Paragraph 1, Point 1 of ZZPL)

You may withdraw your consent at any time without affecting your ability to use the basic functions of the website.

## 5. Data Sharing

We share data exclusively with the following service providers:
- **Vercel** (hosting and analytics) — website visit data
- **Mapbox** (map display) — location data for map rendering

**We do not sell, rent, or share personal user data with third parties for marketing purposes.**

Data may be disclosed to competent authorities solely on the basis of a legal request (court order or request in accordance with the law).

## 6. Cookies

We use a limited number of cookies and local storage technologies. A detailed overview of all cookies, their purposes, and durations can be found on our Cookie Policy page.

Essential cookies are used without consent as they are necessary for the website to function. For analytical cookies, we request consent via the cookie banner.

## 7. User Rights Under ZZPL

In accordance with the Law on Personal Data Protection, you have the following rights:

- **Right of access** — the right to know what data we collect and process about you
- **Right to rectification** — the right to request correction of inaccurate data
- **Right to erasure** — the right to request deletion of your data
- **Right to restriction of processing** — the right to restrict how we use your data
- **Right to object** — the right to object to data processing based on legitimate interest
- **Right to data portability** — the right to receive your data in a structured format

To exercise any of these rights, send a request to: **lazar.cve@gmail.com**

We will respond to your request within 30 days.

**Right to lodge a complaint:** If you believe that the processing of your data is unlawful, you have the right to file a complaint with the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection:
- Website: https://www.poverenik.rs
- Address: Bulevar kralja Aleksandra 15, 11000 Belgrade

## 8. Data Security

We take standard technical and organizational measures to protect data:
- HTTPS encryption for all traffic
- Regular software and dependency updates
- Restricted data access

We do not store user passwords (there is currently no registration system). We do not store payment card information.

While we take reasonable protective measures, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute data security.

## 9. Data Retention Period

- **Analytics data:** up to 26 months (standard analytics retention period)
- **Cookies:** in accordance with durations specified in the Cookie Policy
- **Technical logs:** up to 90 days
- **Newsletter data:** until the user unsubscribes

After the retention period expires, data is automatically deleted or anonymized.

## 10. Children

The Radar Puls service is not intended for persons under the age of 15. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 15. If we learn that we have collected data from a child under 15, we will delete that data as soon as possible.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has used the service, contact us at lazar.cve@gmail.com.
