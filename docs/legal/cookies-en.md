# Cookie Policy

*Last updated: March 12, 2026*

## 1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that websites store on your device (computer, phone, tablet) when you visit a website. They are used to remember your settings, preferences, and anonymous statistical information.

In addition to traditional cookies, we also use localStorage — a similar technology that stores data locally in your browser.

## 2. What Cookies We Use

### Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the basic functioning of the website. We cannot disable them because the website would not work properly without them. They do not require consent.

| Name | Type | Purpose | Duration |
|------|------|---------|----------|
| cookie_consent | localStorage | Stores whether you have accepted cookies | Permanent (until you clear browser data) |
| NEXT_LOCALE | Cookie | Stores selected website language (sr-latn, sr-cyrl, en) | Session |

### Functional Cookies

These cookies remember your choices and preferences for a better user experience.

| Name | Type | Purpose | Duration |
|------|------|---------|----------|
| theme | localStorage | Stores theme selection (light or dark) | Permanent (until you clear browser data) |

### Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how users interact with the website. All data is anonymous. They are only used with your consent.

| Name | Type | Purpose | Duration |
|------|------|---------|----------|
| Vercel Analytics | Third-party | Anonymous visit statistics — visitor count, popular pages, device type | Up to 24h |

## 3. Cookie Categories

### Essential (Always Active)
These cookies are necessary for the website to function and cannot be disabled. They include cookie consent memory and language selection.

### Functional
Cookies that remember your preferences (website theme). You can disable them, but some website features may not work as expected.

### Analytics
Cookies for anonymous website visit analytics. They are activated only after your consent via the cookie banner. You can decline them without affecting your use of the website.

## 4. Managing Cookies

### Via Our Website
When you first visit the website, a cookie banner will appear where you can accept or decline non-essential cookies.

### Via Your Browser
You can also manage cookies in your browser settings:

**Google Chrome:**
Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data

**Mozilla Firefox:**
Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data

**Safari:**
Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data

**Microsoft Edge:**
Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies

**Note:** Deleting or blocking cookies may affect website functionality. Some settings (theme, language) will be reset.

## 5. Third-Party Cookies

We do not control third-party cookies. We recommend visiting their websites for more information.
